What is this site all about?

Hey there! This is a website to help people find new and exciting neovim colorschemes! Om the homepage, you can scroll through our list of the most popular neovim colorschemes. You are welcome to use the search bar to find a colorscheme that you are looking for though.

A note from the creator

My name is Troy. I am a software engineer who has experience with HTML, CSS, TS, Python, and C#. I love learning about new developer tools, and I love open source projects. To help share my passion for software projects like this, I decided to make this site of different Neovim colorschemes. Here you can find a new colorscheme that fits your Neovim setup.
